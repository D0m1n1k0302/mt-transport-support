MT-Transport – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

MT-Transport is a work app for the drivers and administrators of MT Transport. It is used to record deliveries and pick-ups, working time, vehicle use and vacation requests. Accounts are created by the company administrator; there is no public sign-up.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, role (driver/admin) and an internal user ID.
  • Delivery records: place, company name, date and time, notes, recipient name, the recipient's signature and photos of delivery notes and delivered goods that you take or choose in the app.
  • Working time: clock-in and clock-out times, breaks and the vehicle you selected for the day.
  • Location: when you clock in or out, the app asks for permission to read your device's current GPS position once and stores it with that time entry. The app does not track your location in the background. If you deny permission, clocking in and out still works without a location.
  • Vacation requests: requested dates, notes and their status.

How we use it

This data is used only to run the service: to document deliveries for customers, to record working time and vacation for payroll and planning, and to let administrators manage drivers and vehicles. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Where it is stored

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections to the MT-Transport server (mt-transport.app) and stored there. On your device, the app only keeps your sign-in token and your language preference.

Who can see it

Your records can be seen by you and by the company's administrators. Delivery documents may be shared with the customer the delivery belongs to. We do not share data with other third parties unless required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as it is needed for business and legal record-keeping. To access, correct or delete your data, or to have your account removed, contact your administrator or write to us at the address below.
