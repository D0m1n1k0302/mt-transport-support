Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are created by your company administrator. There is no public sign-up.

I forgot my password.
Ask your administrator to reset it.

Why does the app ask for my location?
Your position is saved once when you clock in or out. You can deny the permission; clocking in still works.

Why does the app ask for camera or photo access?
To attach photos of delivery notes and delivered goods to a delivery.

How do I change the language?
Use the DE / EN switch on the sign-in screen, or More → Settings.
